these exercises does not make your solutions AGPL.

## Plain-language summary

*This summary is for orientation only; the formal grant below is what counts.*

- **Your exercise code is yours.** Any code you write to complete the
  curriculum exercises — including code that starts from, modifies, or is
  combined with the exercise starter files we provide — belongs to you. You
  may keep it private, open-source it under any license, sell it, or put it
  in your portfolio. The AGPL does not apply to your own work.
- **The curriculum itself stays AGPL.** This exception does not cover the
  lesson texts, quizzes, teaching notes, canonical solutions, the tutor skill,
  or the tooling. If you redistribute the lesson materials themselves — or
  modified versions of them — the AGPL applies in full.

## Formal grant of additional permission

### 1. Definitions

- **"The Curriculum"** means the `learning` (tutor) project as a whole: the
  curriculum engine and tooling (including `tutor.py` and the tutor skill),
  the registry, the lesson materials (including lesson texts, teaching notes,
  quizzes, rubrics, and canonical solutions), and the Exercise Starter Files.
- **"Exercise Starter Files"** means the files that the Curriculum's
  scaffolding places into a learner's workspace as starting points for
  completing an exercise, including starter source code, test files, and
  exercise scaffolding.
- **"Learner Work"** means a work created by a learner in the course of
  completing, attempting, or extending a curriculum exercise or capstone
  project, including any Exercise Starter Files that the work is derived
  from, incorporates, or is combined with.

### 2. Grant

As an additional permission under section 7 of the AGPL-3.0, you have
permission to use, reproduce, modify, license, sublicense, convey, and
distribute Learner Work, in source or object form, under terms of your own
choosing, without the conditions of the AGPL-3.0 applying to the Learner
Work — notwithstanding that the Learner Work is derived from, incorporates,
or is combined with the Exercise Starter Files.

### 3. Limitations

This exception applies **only** to Learner Work. It does not extend to, and
grants no additional permission for:

1. the lesson materials themselves — lesson texts, teaching notes, quizzes,
   rubrics, canonical solutions, or any curriculum prose;
2. the curriculum engine, the tutor skill, the registry, or any other
   tooling in the Curriculum; or
3. redistribution of the Curriculum, the lesson materials, or the Exercise
   Starter Files as such — that is, conveying them (modified or not) as
   curriculum, teaching, or exercise materials rather than as an
   incorporated part of Learner Work.

Everything listed above remains governed by the AGPL-3.0 without regard to
this exception.
