# Exercise: run the design process on a vague brief

You are the engineer assigned to the brief in [`brief.md`](brief.md). Work
through the four worksheets **in order** — each feeds the next, exactly like
the phases of a design discussion:

1. [`01-requirements.md`](01-requirements.md) — turn the wish into
   requirements and scope.
2. [`02-estimates.md`](02-estimates.md) — put numbers on it and find the
   dominant axis.
3. [`03-tradeoffs.md`](03-tradeoffs.md) — frame two decisions properly.
4. [`04-discussion-plan.md`](04-discussion-plan.md) — assemble the whole
   discussion, architecture sketch included.

Ground rules:

- Fill the worksheets in place — replace every `…` with your answer. Keep
  the tables; add rows freely.
- **Show your arithmetic.** `2M × 20 / 10⁵ ≈ 400/s` earns credit; a bare
  `400/s` does not.
- **Every assumed number gets a one-line justification.** "15% DAU/registered
  — typical for consumer apps" is enough. No silent numbers.
- Rounding is a virtue. Powers of ten and friendly factors; precision
  theater is a smell.
- Where the brief is silent, either write the question you would ask the
  sponsor (worksheet 1 has a section for these) or state an assumption and
  proceed. Never stall.

Budget roughly 90 minutes for all four. When you are done, tell your tutor:
verification is a **design review conversation** against your worksheets.
Expect to defend your numbers and name what would change your mind — that
conversation, not the paperwork, is the gate.
