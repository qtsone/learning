# Decision records

Five to seven records. Copy the template per decision. Rules:

- At least two **real** alternatives — options a competent engineer would
  actually consider. Straw men are worse than nothing; they will be the
  first thing challenged.
- An honest cost for **every** alternative, including the one you picked.
- The pick cites a requirement or estimate **by ID** (N2, A3, …).
- **Consequences** names what the team now lives with — the bill, not the
  benefit.
- A **flip condition** that is concrete. "Nothing would flip it" is an
  automatic redo.

Write records for the decisions you actually agonized over. At least one
must be about the hard axis of your brief; at least one about data
(model, partitioning, or retention); at least one about a failure or
consistency trade-off.
